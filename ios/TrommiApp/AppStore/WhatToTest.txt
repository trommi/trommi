This build is the first one on the new encryption core (MLS, one Rust core for all apps). It has never run on a phone. Expect it to fail early; what matters is WHERE.

Please look at, in this order:
1. Does it start, and do the screens look as before (sign-in, Desk, Settings)? Nothing in the looks was meant to change.
2. Settings, last row "MLS proof": it should say OK with the steps and times. "Copy result" and send it, whatever it says.
3. Creating an account or signing in. This build still speaks the account form of an older hub (e-mail in the field "email"); against today's hub it will most likely be refused. Tell us the exact words on screen.

Known not to work in this build:
- Signing in against the current hub (see 3), and with it everything behind the sign-in.
- Chat, cards, notes, the Scribble Board and pairing a device are wired to the core and pass their tests on Linux, but have never met a real hub.
- Content from before a device joined by link is not shown yet.
- Notifications show the fixed text only ("A new question."), never a title.
- Passkeys are switched off. Lost-device recovery and replacing the recovery code have no screen yet.
