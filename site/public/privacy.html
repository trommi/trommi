Privacy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Trommi is built so that its server cannot read what you and your agents write. This page says what is processed all the same.

Who is responsible

Christopher Eller, a private individual in Germany, is the controller under the GDPR. Contact: trommi@mail101.de; the postal address is in the imprint.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing loaded from another address. If you pick Light or Dark at the top of a page, the site stores that choice in your browser (local storage) and nothing else; it is never sent anywhere. The pages are hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), whose network processes your IP address and your request to deliver them (Cloudflare's privacy policy). Trommi keeps no log of visits.

The app and its server

The app runs on your device and talks to one server, the hub, which it reaches through Cloudflare. The iPhone app comes through Apple's TestFlight for now; Apple handles the install and passes crash reports of testers on to the operator. Claude Code is a product of Anthropic: what your sessions send there is between you and Anthropic.

What the server can read

  • Your email address, a slow hash of a key derived from your password, and the public keys of your passkeys; never the password.
  • Your devices and agents: identifiers, public keys, which of them share a session, who was added or removed and when.
  • For every envelope: the device that sent it and the one it is for, when, its kind (a card, an answer, a chat message, an item on a Scribble Board), what it belongs to and its size.
  • For every card: whether it is open, answered or closed, its urgency and when it was answered.
  • For files and shared links: identifiers, the size of the encrypted file, who uploaded it and what it belongs to, when a link expires.
  • Your push registration, if you turn push on.
  • Whether a device is connected, and whether an agent is working.

What it cannot read: everything you and your agents write. Messages, cards and answers, drawings, the names of sessions, desks and devices, and files with their names are encrypted on your device, and the server holds no key that opens them. The operator cannot read them and cannot recover them for you.

One limit of this: in a browser, the app's code is delivered by the same operator each time you open it. Whoever serves that code could read what the app reads.

The hub uses IP addresses in memory only, to limit sign-in attempts and abuse, and stores none. It keeps an operating log of starts, errors and account events.

Push and email

Push is off until you turn it on. It goes through the push service of your browser's maker (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft) or through Apple's for the iPhone, and carries none of your content: only that something waits or that an agent lost its connection, how urgent it is, and for the iPhone's Live Activity two counts. Your email address is used to sign you in, and for nothing else.

How long data is kept

Your account and your devices are kept until the account is deleted, and so is what you and your agents write, encrypted. Deleting or erasing something in the app does not remove it from the server yet.

Some things the server drops on its own. 30 days after a card, a permission request or an artifact is answered or closed and not opened again, it drops its encrypted text, the chat on a card and its files, and keeps an envelope without content. The steps an agent reports while it works are kept for 30 days. A shared link lasts 180 days at most.

Your rights

Data is processed to provide the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to keep it running and safe (Art. 6(1)(f)). There are no ads and no profiling, and nothing is sold; data goes only to Cloudflare and the push services named above, companies in the USA that rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

You can ask what is stored about you, have it corrected or deleted, have its processing restricted, receive it in a portable form, object, and complain to a data protection supervisory authority. To delete your account, write to the contact above: the account, its devices, keys, envelopes and files are deleted by hand. A single device or agent you can remove yourself in the app.
